Who I Am

Identity is the front door.
I make sure it holds.

Today, I'm IAM Architect & Backend/Security Tech Lead at Lectra, where I design — and continuously evolve, from the ground up — the IDM/IDP platform at the core of Lectra Cloud: over 100 highly available microservices serving the entire R&D organization.

Beyond the architecture itself, I act as a technical referent for identity and access management — guiding R&D teams through the OAuth2 and OIDC flows they rely on every day, and translating IAM stakes into value that non-technical stakeholders can act on.

I see that transmission role as just as critical as the technical design work itself.
